Part of the Moychay network

Moychay Phuket is part of Moychay, an international tea culture company founded in 2009 in Amsterdam. Moychay works directly with tea farmers, tea factories and artisan workshops, places a strong emphasis on quality control and sells only natural loose-leaf tea.

For your business this means a range built on direct sourcing, and a local team on Boat Avenue that brews, serves and talks about tea every day.
