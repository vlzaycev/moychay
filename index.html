A quiet place in Bangtao

You don't need to know anything about tea to come in. Some guests book a ceremony, some want a quiet corner away from the beach, some come with a laptop for a matcha latte, and some just want to see what a tea house is like.

Bare concrete, calligraphy on the walls, a rattan peacock chair by the window and shelves of teapots – most of them for sale. The tea master will help you choose: a full ceremony or simply a good cup.

  • 200+ teas to taste
  • Antique teaware – for sale, not decor
  • Board games and a reading corner
  • We speak Thai, English and Russian

Tea ceremonies

  • Walk-in tasting (Pin Cha)

    300 ฿ + tea by weight

    A 10-minute introduction from the tea master, then you stay with the tea you picked. No booking needed.

  • Gong fu cha ceremony

    600 ฿ per person

    60 minutes with the tea master: brewing steps, teaware and the tea itself, then up to an hour to stay and relax. Booking required.

  • Group ceremonies

    Price on request

    Birthdays, team events, gatherings – up to 30 guests, at the tea house or at your venue.

  • Matcha drinks & kombucha

    from 250 ฿

    Non-alcoholic matcha lattes and tonics, kombucha on tap. All available to go.

Not sure where to start? Ask the tea master – from a light green tea to an aged pu-erh.

Our teas

No need to know any of this before you come. Point at a jar, and the tea master will brew it for you.

  • White tea

    Light and delicate, with notes of honey and dried flowers.

  • Green tea

    Fresh and grassy, sometimes brothy, always bright.

  • Light oolongs

    Soft, floral and sweet – halfway between green and black tea.

  • Dark oolongs

    Roasted and rich, with notes of dark chocolate and toasted nuts.

  • Red tea (what the West calls black tea)

    Warming and smooth, with dried fruit and soft spice.

  • Young raw pu-erh (sheng)

    Fresh, vivid and a little grassy – energizing and clean.

  • Aged raw pu-erh (sheng)

    The same tea, matured for 10+ years into something deep and complex.

  • Shu pu-erh & dark tea

    Earthy and grounding – a good match after a heavy meal.

  • Lao cha (aged tea)

    Years of careful storage turn bitterness into smooth, layered depth.

  • Herbal tea

    Caffeine-free infusions of herbs, flowers and fruit – calming or energizing.

Moychay Phuket

Open on Boat Avenue since 2024. We're part of Moychay, an international tea community with tea houses around the world, so our teas come through suppliers they've worked with for years.

The tea house itself is small: locals and travellers share the same table, and the tea master is here to explain, not to upsell.
