A quiet place in Bangtao

You don't need to know anything about tea to come in. Guests book a ceremony, work over a matcha latte, or just want a quiet corner away from the beach.

Concrete walls, calligraphy, a rattan peacock chair and shelves of teapots – most of them for sale. The tea master will help you choose.

  • 200+ teas to taste
  • Antique teaware – for sale, not decor
  • Board games and a reading corner
  • We speak Thai, English and Russian

Our teas

No need to know any of this before you come. Point at a jar, and the tea master will brew it for you.

  • White tea

    Light and delicate, with notes of honey and dried flowers.

  • Green tea

    Fresh and grassy, sometimes brothy, always bright.

  • Light oolongs

    Soft, floral and sweet – halfway between green and black tea.

  • Dark oolongs

    Roasted and rich, with notes of dark chocolate and toasted nuts.

  • Red tea (what the West calls black tea)

    Warming and smooth, with dried fruit and soft spice.

  • Young raw pu-erh (sheng)

    Fresh, vivid and a little grassy – energizing and clean.

  • Aged raw pu-erh (sheng)

    The same tea, matured for 10+ years into something deep and complex.

  • Shu pu-erh & dark tea

    Earthy and grounding – a good match after a heavy meal.

  • Lao cha (aged tea)

    Years of careful storage turn bitterness into smooth, layered depth.

  • Herbal tea

    Caffeine-free infusions of herbs, flowers and fruit – calming or energizing.
