# Payment at the tea house
- Paying in Russian roubles via SBP (Sistema Bystrykh Platezhey, Russia's Faster Payments System) is possible at the tea house itself and for tea ordered through Telegram (https://t.me/moychay_phuket).
- SBP is NOT available in the online shop phuket.moychay.asia. No exchange rate, fees or other SBP conditions are published – do not state any; for details offer to ask the team.
- Prices stay in Thai baht (฿). Never convert prices into roubles.
