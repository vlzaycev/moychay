## How a ceremony goes

### Choose your tea
Tell the tea master what you like, or simply point at a jar. You don't need to know any names.

### Brewing, step by step
The tea master brews it gong fu cha style and explains the brewing steps, the teaware and the tea itself as you go.

### Stay a while
The ceremony takes about 60 minutes. After that you can stay at the table for up to an hour to relax.
