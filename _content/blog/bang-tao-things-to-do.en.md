Bang Tao is one of Phuket's calmest and most convenient areas: a long beach on the island's northwest coast, the Laguna Phuket lagoons, the restaurants and shops of Boat Avenue, and none of the crowds of Patong. Our tea house is here, on Boat Avenue, so these are the places we go ourselves and recommend to guests.

## Bang Tao at a glance

| If you want | Go to |
|---|---|
| Sea and sunset | Bang Tao beach, the quieter north end and Layan |
| A party | The beach clubs |
| Food and a stroll | Boat Avenue and Porto de Phuket |
| To unwind | Spas in Laguna and on Srisoonthon Road |
| Sport | Muay Thai, surfing, SUP, golf |
| Calm and something new | A tea ceremony at Moychay Phuket |

## Beach and sea

### 1. Bang Tao Beach

One of Phuket's longest beaches: several kilometres of sand from Laguna in the south to Layan in the north. The south end has hotels and beach clubs, the north is quieter. Sunset is the best time, as the beach faces west. In the rainy season (roughly May to October) waves can be strong – stay out when red flags are up.

### 2. Layan Beach

A small, quiet beach north of Bang Tao next to the national park. Good for a walk and a calm evening.

### 3. Surfing and SUP

In wave season surf schools run lessons on Bang Tao, and on calm days you can rent a stand-up paddleboard and paddle towards Surin.

### 4. Sunset horse riding

Riding along Bang Tao beach is one of the most photogenic things to do in the area. Local clubs run the rides; book ahead.

## Evenings and nightlife

### 5. Beach clubs

Bang Tao has some of Phuket's best-known beach clubs: Catch Beach Club, Carpe Diem, Xana Beach Club at Angsana and others. Pools and loungers by day, DJs and cocktails at night. Book a sunset table.

### 6. Boat Avenue and Porto de Phuket

The area's "town centre": cafés, restaurants, bars, Villa Market for imported groceries, shops and parking. On Friday evenings there is a night market with street food. Our tea house is here too, on the 2nd floor.

## Rest and self-care

### 7. Spa and massage

The Laguna Phuket hotels have spas, including the famous Banyan Tree spa, and more affordable massage places line Srisoonthon Road and the area around Boat Avenue. An hour's massage is perfect for a hot midday or a rain shower.

### 8. Yoga

There are plenty of yoga studios and retreats nearby; classes are often in the morning, before the heat.

## Sport and activities

### 10. Muay Thai

Bang Tao has several well-known Muay Thai gyms, and many take drop-in sessions, including beginners.

### 11. Golf and mini golf

Laguna Golf Phuket and Phuket Country Club are nearby, and families can try Phuket Adventure Mini Golf, which has a restaurant too.

### 12. Kayaking in the lagoon

The Laguna Phuket lagoons offer calm water for kayaks and boat rides, and free shuttle boats run between the hotels for guests.

## Culture and nature

### 13. Cherngtalay temple and Chinese shrine

The local Buddhist temple, Wat Cherngtalay, and the Chinese shrine nearby are quiet places to see everyday life away from the tourist trail. Cover shoulders and knees.

### 14. Elephants

Not far from Bang Tao there are elephant parks where you feed and watch the animals instead of riding them. Choose ethical, no-riding parks.

### 15. Blue Tree

An entertainment complex in Cherngtalay with water and sports activities and a kids' area. Check what is open on the day before you go.

## Getting there and around

Bang Tao is roughly 20-30 minutes by car from the airport. Within the area, taxis and Grab are easiest, and shuttles run between the Laguna hotels. Boat Avenue is a handy meeting point, with parking and everything close by.

## FAQ

### Is Bang Tao good for families?

Yes: a wide beach, a calm lagoon, mini golf, Blue Tree and the cafés of Boat Avenue. In wave season, watch the beach flags.

### Where can I spend a quiet evening in Bang Tao?

Sunset at the north end of the beach, dinner on Boat Avenue and a tea ceremony – we are open daily until 22:00.

### What to do in Bang Tao when it rains?

Spa, a workout, Boat Avenue and the tea house. More ideas in [rainy day in Bang Tao](/blog/rainy-day-in-bang-tao/).
