Rain in Phuket rarely ruins a whole day. Usually it is a short, heavy downpour, especially in the rainy season from May to October, and then the sun comes back. But you still need somewhere to spend an hour or two. Here is what to do in Bang Tao and Cherngtalay without going far.

## The main rule

Don't drive across the island to escape the rain. The shower will probably be over before you get there, and roads get slippery in the rain, especially on a scooter. Better to wait it out nearby. And stay out of the sea when red flags are up: in the rainy season the west coast can get big waves.

## Boat Avenue and Porto de Phuket

The two main shopping areas here, right next to each other. Cafés and restaurants, supermarkets, pharmacies, shops, covered walkways and their own parking. The easiest option if the rain catches you in Bang Tao.

## Spa and massage

You will find spas and massage places along Srisoonthon Road, around Boat Avenue and in the Laguna Phuket hotels. An hour's massage is about the right length for a tropical shower.

## A workout

Bang Tao has plenty of gyms and Muay Thai camps. A session indoors is a good use of a rainy morning. Many gyms take drop-in visitors, but check the schedule first.

## A cooking class

A Thai cooking class takes a few hours, happens under a roof, and ends with you eating what you cooked. Classes run across the island, including in hotels on the northwest coast.

## A tea ceremony

Our tea house, Moychay Phuket, is on the 2nd floor of Boat Avenue. It is especially cosy in the rain: concrete walls, calligraphy, a round table and shelves of teapots.

- **Walk-in tasting.** 300฿ plus tea by weight: about 10 minutes with the tea master, then you stay with your tea as long as you like.
- **Gong fu cha ceremony.** 600฿ per person, 60 minutes plus up to an hour to relax. Book on WhatsApp.
- **If you just want to sit it out.** Matcha lattes and tonics from 250฿, kombucha on tap, board games and a reading corner.

Open daily 11:00-22:00, parking at Boat Avenue. More about the formats in [tea ceremony in Phuket](/blog/tea-ceremony-in-phuket/).

## If it rains all day

Then head to Central Phuket, the big shopping centre near Phuket Town, roughly 30-40 minutes by car from Bang Tao. It has shops, a cinema, restaurants and the Aquaria Phuket aquarium. Or spend the day in Phuket Old Town: cafés, museums and small shops in old Sino-Portuguese houses.

## When the rain stops

After a shower the beach is empty and the light is beautiful. Time to head back to the sea.
