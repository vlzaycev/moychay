Phuket's beaches are wonderful, but after a few days you want something else. Here are 12 ways to spend a day differently on the island, from temples and the Old Town to a cooking class and a tea ceremony.

## Culture and history

### 1. Big Buddha

A huge marble statue on a hilltop in the south of the island and one of Phuket's symbols, with views over Chalong and Kata bays. Cover shoulders and knees.

### 2. Wat Chalong

The island's most revered Buddhist temple. Beautiful architecture and a calm atmosphere, especially in the morning.

### 3. Phuket Old Town

Streets of Sino-Portuguese houses, cafés, galleries and small museums. Best explored in the morning or late afternoon; on Sunday evenings a walking-street market opens here.

## Views

### 4. Khao Rang viewpoint

A hill next to Phuket Town with views over the city and the bay, and a café at the top.

### 5. Promthep Cape

The famous sunset spot at the island's southern tip. More calm evening ideas in [things to do in Phuket at night](/blog/phuket-quiet-evening/).

## Try something new

### 6. A Thai cooking class

A few hours and you can make tom yum and pad thai. Classes run all over the island.

### 7. Muay Thai

A session at a Muay Thai gym is a way to experience local culture from the inside. Many gyms welcome beginners for drop-in classes.

### 8. A tea ceremony

At Moychay Phuket in Bang Tao you can spend an hour with a tea master who brews Chinese tea gong fu cha style and explains what is happening in your cup. No experience needed – most guests try it for the first time. A ceremony is 600฿ per person; a walk-in tasting is 300฿ plus tea by weight.

More in [tea ceremony in Phuket](/blog/tea-ceremony-in-phuket/) and [what happens at a Chinese tea ceremony](/blog/what-happens-at-a-chinese-tea-ceremony/).

### 9. Workshops and meetups

We hold tea evenings, tastings and workshops every week, sometimes with calligraphy and teaware. See what's on in our [events](/events/).

## Rest and nature

### 10. Spa and Thai massage

Phuket has spas for every budget, from small massage shops to hotel spas. Perfect for a hot midday.

### 11. Ethical elephant parks

The island has parks where elephants are fed and observed rather than ridden. Choose parks without riding or shows.

### 12. Day trips

From Phuket it is easy to visit Phang Nga Bay, the Phi Phi islands or Krabi. It takes a full day, so book ahead and check the weather.

## FAQ

### What can I see in Phuket on my own?

Big Buddha, Wat Chalong, the Old Town, Khao Rang and Promthep Cape are all easy without a tour, by taxi or rental car.

### What are unique things to do in Phuket?

A cooking class, Muay Thai, yoga and a tea ceremony – each takes a few hours and you leave with a new skill.
