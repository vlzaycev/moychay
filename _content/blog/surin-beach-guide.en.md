Surin is a small, very pretty beach on Phuket's north-west coast, between Kamala and Bang Tao: soft sand, shady trees, turquoise water in season and villas on the hills. Our tea house is in neighbouring Bang Tao, about 10 minutes away by car, so here's what we recommend to guests staying in Surin.

## The beach

Surin Beach is about 800 metres long, with a steep entry into the water and trees along the shore for shade. It faces west, so sunset is the main event of the day.

- **When to swim.** From November to April the sea is usually calm and clear. From May to October there can be big waves and strong currents – don't swim when the red flags are up.
- **Snorkelling.** On calm days you can see fish around the rocks at either end of the beach.
- **Food.** Check what's open on the beach during your season and bring water.

## Neighbouring beaches

### Pansea

A small, secluded beach just north of Surin, past the rocks. It's lined with resorts, so it's almost always quiet.

### Laem Singh

A small, scenic cove between Surin and Kamala, framed by rocks. Access to the beach has changed several times, so check how to get there before you go. The viewpoint on the road above the bay is worth a stop.

### Kamala

A quiet, family-friendly beach south of Surin, also home to the evening shows and theme parks families often ask about.

### Bang Tao

The long beach to the north, with beach clubs, the Laguna Phuket lagoons and Boat Avenue. Full guide: [things to do in Bang Tao](/blog/bang-tao-things-to-do/).

## Things to do

### Sunset

Surin is one of the best sunset spots on the west coast. Come early and bring something to sit on.

### Spa and massage

The road between Surin and Bang Tao is full of massage shops, and the resorts have pricier spas. A good plan for a hot afternoon.

### Sport

Muay Thai, yoga, surfing in the wave season and SUP on calm days – all available in Surin and next door in Bang Tao.

### Boat Avenue

The nearest "town centre" to Surin, with restaurants, cafes, shops, a supermarket and parking. On Friday evenings there's a night market with street food.

### A tea ceremony

At Moychay Phuket, on the second floor of Boat Avenue, the tea master brews Chinese tea gong fu cha style and explains it as he goes. No experience needed.

- **Walk-in tasting** – 300฿ plus tea by weight.
- **Gong fu cha ceremony** – 600฿ per person, 60 minutes plus up to an hour to relax, booked on WhatsApp.
- **Open daily** 11:00–22:00.

## If it rains

Spa, a workout, lunch on Boat Avenue and the tea house. More ideas in [rainy day in Bang Tao](/blog/rainy-day-in-bang-tao/).

## Getting there

Surin is roughly 25–35 minutes from the airport by car. Taxis and Grab are the easiest way around. Boat Avenue is about 10 minutes away by car, and Patong about 30 minutes, depending on traffic.

## FAQ

### Can I swim at Surin in the rainy season?

Watch the flags. When the waves are big, a pool or the calm Laguna Phuket lagoons are the safer choice.

### Where can I spend a quiet evening near Surin?

Sunset on the beach, dinner on Boat Avenue and a tea ceremony. More ideas in [things to do in Phuket at night](/blog/phuket-quiet-evening/).
