## How to choose tea without tasting it

Ordering tea blind can feel risky, so a few tips:

- **Start with small packs** of different teas rather than one big cake.
- **Tell us what you like.** Coffee, dark chocolate, flowers, fruit – it is easy to match a tea to that.
- **Ask the tea master.** We reply on WhatsApp and explain how to brew it.

## Storing tea in Bangkok

Bangkok is hot and humid, and tea easily absorbs moisture and smells. Keep it tightly sealed, in a dark place, away from the kitchen, spices and coffee. Pu-erh cakes like stable conditions: don't keep them next to the air conditioner or on a sunny windowsill.
