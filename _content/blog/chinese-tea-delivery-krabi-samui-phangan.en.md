On the islands and in resort towns, good loose-leaf tea is even harder to find than in Bangkok: shops sell tea bags and souvenir tins, and specialist tea houses are rare. Moychay Phuket ships tea across Thailand, including to Krabi, Koh Samui and Koh Phangan.

## Who we are

Moychay Phuket is a tea house on Boat Avenue in Bang Tao, Phuket, and part of the international tea company Moychay. Since 2009 Moychay has worked directly with more than 700 suppliers, farmers and workshops in China, Japan, Vietnam, Thailand and Georgia. We sell only natural loose-leaf tea with no artificial flavourings.

## Krabi

Ao Nang, Railay and Klong Muang are places for slow mornings and sunset evenings. Soft oolongs and white tea suit those days, and shu pu-erh is great for cooler rainy-season evenings.

## Koh Samui

Many people on Samui stay for months: yoga, retreats, remote work. For them we usually suggest an everyday tea – an oolong or red tea – and for practice and meditation, sheng pu-erh and caffeine-free herbal blends.

## Koh Phangan

Phangan is an island of retreats, meditation and a slower pace. A home tea practice fits well: a gaiwan, a couple of cups and a few teas for daily gong fu cha. We explain how in [how to brew oolong at home](/blog/how-to-brew-oolong/).

## Storing tea in the tropics

The islands are hot and humid. Keep tea tightly sealed, in a dark place, away from the kitchen and spices. Pu-erh cakes like stable conditions – keep them away from the air conditioner and direct sun.
