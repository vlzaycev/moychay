Oolong is one of the most rewarding teas to brew at home. It forgives mistakes, opens up over many infusions and comes in many styles, from light and floral to dark and roasted. Here is how to brew it well without complicated rules.

## Types of oolong

Oolong is a partly oxidised tea, somewhere between green and red. For brewing, two types matter:

- **Light oolongs.** Rolled into balls, soft and floral. Brew them with water just below boiling.
- **Dark oolongs.** Roasted, often with long twisted leaves. Notes of chocolate, nuts and bread. Brew them with boiling water.

## What you need

- A 100-150 ml gaiwan or a small teapot. A regular teapot works too.
- A pitcher to pour the tea into, and small cups. A mug will do.
- Good water: filtered or bottled. Water is almost half the taste.

## Brewing in a gaiwan

1. **Warm the teaware.** Rinse the gaiwan and cups with hot water.
2. **Add the tea.** About 5-7 g per 100 ml. For rolled oolong, enough to cover the bottom in one layer.
3. **Rinse the leaves.** Pour hot water over them and pour it straight off. This helps the leaves open.
4. **First infusion.** Add water and pour off the tea after 10-20 seconds.
5. **Next infusions.** Add 5-10 seconds each time.

| Oolong | Water temperature | First infusions | Number of infusions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 90-95°C | 10-20 seconds | 6-8 |
| Dark, roasted | 95-100°C | 10-15 seconds | 5-7 |

If the tea turns bitter, shorten the infusion. If it is too weak, steep longer or add more leaves.

## Brewing in a regular teapot

No gaiwan? Use 3-4 g of oolong for 250-300 ml of water at the right temperature and steep for 2-3 minutes. You can re-steep the leaves 2-3 more times, a little longer each time.

## Cold brew

Perfect for Phuket heat. Put 8-10 g of light oolong into a litre of cold water and leave it in the fridge for 6-8 hours. You get a soft, slightly sweet tea with no bitterness.

## How to store it

Keep oolong in a tightly closed jar or bag, in a dark place, away from spices, coffee and strong smells. In Phuket's humid climate an airtight pack matters even more.
