Most celebration ideas in Phuket involve a yacht, a villa with a DJ or a beach club. Sometimes you want something else: one table, real conversation and an evening you remember rather than a morning you regret. A group tea ceremony is exactly that.

## Who it's for

- **Birthdays** – for people who want to celebrate calmly, somewhere beautiful, without alcohol.
- **Team events and team building** – a company offsite, a team on a workation, a client meeting.
- **Friends and family** – when everyone has finally made it to the island and you want an evening together.
- **Hotel guests and residents** – as part of a guest programme.
- **Retreats and yoga groups** – a quiet practice that sits well next to the classes.

## How a group ceremony works

The tea master brews Chinese tea gong fu cha style: several short infusions of one tea, then another, so you can taste the difference. Along the way he explains where the tea comes from, how it's made and why it's brewed this way. Guests taste, ask questions and talk – there's no strict ritual and no enforced silence.

No experience needed: most guests are trying Chinese tea for the first time. If someone in the group avoids caffeine, tell us in advance.

More about what happens at the tea table – in [what happens at a Chinese tea ceremony](/blog/what-happens-at-a-chinese-tea-ceremony/).

## Where

### At our tea house on Boat Avenue

Our tea house is on the second floor of Boat Avenue in Bang Tao: a long wooden table, soft light and candles in the evening. We can host groups of up to 30 guests. There's parking on site, and dinner at one of the Boat Avenue restaurants is a short walk away.

### At your venue

We hold ceremonies and tastings at villas, hotels, offices and events. We bring the tea and the teaware, and the tea master hosts in English or Russian.

## What we agree in advance

- the date and time;
- the number of guests;
- the place: our tea house or your venue;
- the length and format: a short tasting or a full ceremony;
- the language: English, Russian or Thai;
- teaware, space and travel if the ceremony is at your venue.

The price of a group ceremony depends on your request. Message us on WhatsApp with the date, the number of guests and the place, and we'll reply with the details and the price.

## Why it beats a regular dinner

- **Everyone takes part.** Guests taste, compare and talk instead of splitting into corners.
- **Alcohol-free.** Easy when the group includes kids, athletes, people who are pregnant or simply don't drink.
- **Memorable.** For many guests it's their first real Chinese tea.
- **A gift to take home.** Any tea people liked can be bought at the tea house or in the online store.
