Phuket nightlife usually means Bangla Road in Patong: neon, music and bars until morning. But plenty of people want a different kind of evening – beautiful, calm, without loud music or alcohol. Here are ideas for couples, families and anyone who has had enough noise.

## Sunset

### Promthep Cape

The island's best-known viewpoint, in the far south, where people come to watch the sun drop into the sea. Arrive early – it gets busy at sunset.

### West-coast beaches

The whole west coast faces the sunset: Karon, Kata, Kamala, Surin, Bang Tao. The north end of Bang Tao and Layan beach are among the calmest places to watch it without a crowd.

### Beach clubs at sunset

For something stylish but not a late party, go to a beach club for sunset rather than midnight. There are several on Bang Tao and Surin; book a table.

## Night markets

### Old Town Sunday walking street

On Sunday evenings a pedestrian street opens in Phuket Old Town with street food, souvenirs and music – and the old Sino-Portuguese houses lit up.

### Weekend night market

At weekends a big night market near Phuket Town sells food, clothes and everything else. Come hungry.

### Friday market at Boat Avenue

In the northwest of the island, a Friday-evening night market with street food runs at Boat Avenue in Bang Tao – right next to our tea house.

## Shows

Phuket has plenty of evening shows: cabaret, Muay Thai at the stadiums, cultural shows with dance. Book ahead and check whether a show suits children.

## A calm evening at a tea house

A tea ceremony is one of the quietest evenings you can spend in Phuket. Soft light, a round table, and a tea master brewing Chinese tea and explaining it as you go. No alcohol, no music, no rush.

- **Moychay Phuket**, 2nd floor, Boat Avenue, Bang Tao.
- **Open daily until 22:00** – there is time after dinner.
- **Walk-in tasting** – 300฿ plus tea by weight.
- **Gong fu cha ceremony** – 600฿ per person, 60 minutes plus up to an hour to relax. Book on WhatsApp.
- **Not a tea person?** Non-alcoholic matcha drinks and kombucha on tap.

## For couples

Sunset on the beach, dinner on Boat Avenue and a tea ceremony for two – a calm, memorable evening without the rush.

## For families

Night markets, an early dinner and the tea house: kids love watching tea being brewed, and there are matcha drinks and kombucha for those who don't drink tea.

## FAQ

### What to do in Phuket at night without drinking?

Sunset viewpoints, night markets, the Old Town and a tea house. A tea ceremony is a full evening out with no alcohol.

### What to do in Bang Tao in the evening?

Sunset on the beach, dinner on Boat Avenue, the Friday night market and Moychay Phuket tea house. More in [things to do in Bang Tao](/blog/bang-tao-things-to-do/).
