Chiang Mai and neighbouring Chiang Rai are Thailand's tea country. Thai oolongs and northern teas are easy to find at local markets and in the hills. Good Chinese tea – pu-erh, oolong, white and red tea from China – takes more searching. Moychay Phuket ships this kind of tea across Thailand, including to Chiang Mai.

## What we add to Thai tea

Northern Thai teas are excellent, especially Mae Salong oolongs and [GABA oolong](/blog/gaba-oolong/) – see our [Thai tea guide](/blog/thai-tea-guide/). But the Chinese tea tradition is much wider. You can order:

- **Pu-erh.** Young and aged sheng, shu pu-erh and dark tea, pressed and loose.
- **Oolong**, from light and floral to dark and roasted.
- **White tea** – soft, with notes of honey and dried flowers.
- **Red tea** – what the West calls black tea.
- **Aged tea** and caffeine-free **herbal blends**.
- **Teaware** – gaiwans, clay teapots, cups.

## Storing tea in the north

Chiang Mai's climate is milder than the south, but humidity is high in the rainy season. Keep tea tightly sealed, in a dark place and away from kitchen smells. Pu-erh cakes do best in stable conditions without sharp temperature swings.
