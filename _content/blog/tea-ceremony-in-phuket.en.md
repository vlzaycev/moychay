A tea ceremony is one of the calmest ways to spend a couple of hours in Phuket. You don't need to travel far, know anything about tea or prepare in advance. You sit down at the table, the tea master brews tea in front of you and explains what is happening in your cup.

Here are the formats at our tea house, Moychay Phuket in Bangtao, who each one suits and how to book.

## Three formats

| Format | Time | Booking | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walk-in tasting (Pin Cha) | About 10 minutes, then you stay with your tea | Not needed | 300฿ + tea by weight |
| Gong fu cha ceremony | 60 minutes + up to 60 minutes to relax | Required | 600฿ per person |
| Group ceremony | Agreed with you | Required | On request |

### Walk-in tasting

Good for a first look at Chinese tea, or if you are at Boat Avenue anyway. The tea master spends about 10 minutes on the tea you picked, then you stay with it as long as you like. The tea is charged by weight.

### Gong fu cha ceremony

The main format. An hour with the tea master, who brews tea in short gong fu cha infusions and explains every step, the teaware and the tea itself. Afterwards you can stay at the table for up to another hour. A good choice for a first meeting with tea, a date, time with friends or simply a quiet evening. We explain the ceremony itself step by step in [our gong fu cha guide](/blog/what-happens-at-a-chinese-tea-ceremony/).

### Group ceremony

Up to 30 guests, at the tea house or at your venue: birthdays, team events, gatherings. Send us the date, the number of guests and the place, and we will reply with the details and the price. For hotels and companies we also run [tea experiences for businesses](/wholesale/).

## Do you need any experience

No. Most guests try a tea ceremony for the first time. You don't need to know any tea names: tell the tea master what you like, or just point at a jar. Our tea master is Igor, from Astrakhan, who worked in tea houses before Moychay Phuket.

## How to book

The quickest way is WhatsApp. Gong fu cha and group ceremonies need booking, since space at the tea table is limited. For a walk-in tasting just come by during opening hours.

Ceremonies are held in Thai, English and Russian.

## Practical details

- **Where.** Moychay Phuket, 2nd floor, Boat Avenue, Bangtao. Parking on site.
- **When.** Open daily 11:00-22:00.
- **How long to plan.** About two hours for gong fu cha if you want to stay after the ceremony.
- **What else.** Board games, a reading corner, non-alcoholic matcha drinks and kombucha on tap.
- **If you like the tea.** Buy it at the tea house or in our [online store](https://phuket.moychay.asia/) with delivery across Thailand.

We hold tastings and tea evenings every week. See what's on in our [events](/events/).
